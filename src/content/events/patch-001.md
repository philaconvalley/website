Philly, a DJ set can sound alive while the screen behind it sits still. PATCH 001 started with that
problem: the music was alive, but the visuals weren't.

Everyone came in through one of three scenes:

- **Sound.** Drum machines, a DJ controller, and GarageBand. You bring the signal.
- **Visuals.** Sketching and design on tablets and laptops. You make it move.
- **Code.** [p5.js](https://p5js.org) and the Web Audio API. You wire the sound to the visuals.

After a short lesson in each scene, people formed mixed teams with all three scenes at the table.
Each team built a live audio-reactive piece, where what you hear becomes what you see. At the end,
the teams showed their work on the big screen.

The visualizer from that night is open source:
[djVisualizer](https://github.com/philaconvalley/djVisualizer). PATCH 001 was the first night of the
series, and the reason every PATCH since ends with something you made.

Waskar Paulino led the night. Diego Mendoza-Ramos made the photo recap.
