It listens to live audio input from your hardware (a controller, a mixer, or a microphone) and turns it into projected visuals in real time. It does not play audio files: you plug in, press Start, and it reads the room.

See the [repository](https://github.com/philaconvalley/djVisualizer) to run it or contribute.
